# The inquiry is the only unit, and the tree is recursive

The workspace template had three kinds of repository: a hub that piloted, studies that answered, and the research agent template they grew from. A research area does not split that way: a broad question is answered by narrower ones, which split again, and the right depth is only known as the work goes. So there is one kind of repository, the **inquiry**: it owns one question, at any breadth, and may grow sub-inquiries or be adopted into a broader inquiry. The tree of inquiries is the **knowledge tree**. A broad question is answered by decomposing it into sub-inquiries, plus the inquiry's own synthesis; a non-leaf inquiry runs its own phases only for work no child owns (integration, cross-cutting comparison), and a phase whose question fits under one child's belongs in that child.

## Considered options

- **study**: familiar, but it means a bounded piece of work; a research area at the root is not a study. A word tied to a role (programme / study) was rejected too: adopt and detach change a node's role, and its name would have to change with it.
- **node**: true at every scale, but says nothing about research.

## Consequences

No hub, no `workspace.yaml`, no `ws`. A user starts with one inquiry, as with the old template, and grows the tree from either side.
