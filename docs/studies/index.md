# Studies

Every study of the workspace, one line each, as listed in `workspace.yaml`; `ws sync` reports a study missing here, and `ws spawn` adds its line. On a machine with the study cloned, `ws sync` mounts its `docs/` beside this note as `docs/studies/<name>/`, a local link git ignores: cite a study from a committed note by permalink, never through its mount.
