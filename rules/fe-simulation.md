# fe-simulation

For a study whose results come from a finite element model, whether it is the method under study or the reference another method is compared with.

1. **Mesh convergence** of every reported quantity: at least three meshes, refined by a stated ratio; the observed order of convergence; Richardson extrapolation or the grid convergence index (GCI) where the quantity converges monotonically. The headline is given with its discretisation uncertainty (core rule 5).
2. **Solver verification** before results: a patch test, and a case with an analytical solution in the regime of the study (a thick-walled cylinder, a laminated plate), reproduced to a stated tolerance (core rule 2).
3. **Same constitutive law on both sides** of a comparison: the same material model and parameters, from the same source, in the method and in its reference.
4. **Boundary conditions and load** stated in the phase note and checked in the run: the reactions balance the applied load to a stated tolerance, and the constraints remove the rigid-body modes and no more.
5. **Solver version and input deck kept**: the stamp records the solver and its version (through the run's environment or `stamp_extras`), and the input deck is written into the run's folder, or generated by committed code from the run's configuration.
